SIP simulator

Simulates a monthly SIP into a plan (blended by its picks/target, same as elsewhere — a pick not yet launched at a given month is skipped and its share redistributed) from a chosen start date to today, and the same SIP into each benchmark for comparison. Also shows a hypothetical exit tax: what you'd owe (and keep) if everything held were sold today, treating a benchmark as a single equity holding — Sec 112A's ₹1.25L/FY equity LTCG exemption included.

SIP + periodic rebalancing

Same monthly SIP simulation, but every N years the plan's holdings are sold and rebought in the current target proportions instead of just left to drift (leave the interval blank to skip rebalancing entirely and just use the harvesting option below). Each rebalance sale is taxed on its gain (FIFO lot by lot): 12.5% long-term / 20% short-term, with "long-term" meaning held over 12 months for equity classes or over 24 months for everything else. The first ₹1.25L of equity long-term gains each financial year (Apr–Mar) is tax-exempt (Sec 112A), tracked across all of a plan's rebalances that year. A loss on one fund offsets a gain on another the same rebalance first (short-term losses offset anything; long-term losses offset only long-term gains); whatever's left over is carried forward to reduce gains at a later rebalance or in the final exit-tax estimate below, for up to 8 assessment years, same as the real carryforward rule. Unrealized (never-sold) gains aren't taxed along the way. Benchmarks are a single fund each, so rebalancing/tax-loss-carry doesn't apply to them — they're shown as plain buy-and-hold, though they do still get their own hypothetical exit-tax estimate below for a fair comparison.

LTCG harvesting (independent of rebalancing, combine both or use either alone): once a year, sells and immediately rebuys just enough equity long-term units to use up whatever's left of that year's ₹1.25L exemption — zero tax, but it resets those units' cost basis (and 12-month clock) to today, so less gain is left to tax later. Doesn't touch units held, weights, or non-equity classes.
